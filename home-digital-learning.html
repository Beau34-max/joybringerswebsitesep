Safeguarding first

Your child's safety is never an afterthought.

Joybringers is a safeguarding-led organisation. Every part of the Home Digital Learning programme is designed with child protection at its core.

DBS-checked tutors

Every Joybringers tutor is DBS-checked and has completed safeguarding training before their very first session. No exceptions.

Parent present

A parent or responsible adult must be at home and available throughout every session. This is a non-negotiable safeguarding requirement.

Lone-working policy

Tutors follow Joybringers lone-working and home-visit procedures. Safeguarding concerns are escalated immediately.

Questions

Parents ask us…

Who are the tutors and how are they checked?

All Joybringers tutors are DBS-checked, safeguarding-trained and matched to learners based on subject specialism, experience with that age group and proximity to your home. We never send a tutor who has not completed our induction process.

Does a parent need to be home during the session?

Yes, always. A parent or responsible adult must be available in the home throughout every session. This is a non-negotiable safeguarding requirement, not just a suggestion.

Can siblings learn together to save money?

Yes — sibling sessions are one of the most popular options. Up to three children at the same address can share a 90-minute session at £65, which works out significantly cheaper per learner than individual sessions. Groups of up to four from £20–£25 per child are also available.

Which areas do you cover?

We are launching in Newcastle and surrounding North East areas. If you are not sure whether we cover your postcode yet, register anyway — we are expanding tutor coverage based on demand and you will be contacted as soon as we reach your area.

My family cannot afford the full price. Can we still apply?

Please do. Joybringers offers subsidised and fully-funded places for eligible families. We assess this case by case and there is no judgement involved. Just tick "subsidised place" on the form or mention it in your message.

How does this help other young people?

Joybringers is a charity. The surplus from paid home-learning sessions funds free digital training for disadvantaged young people across the North East who would not otherwise have access. When you book a paid session, you are investing in your child and helping us reach another young person who needs it most.
