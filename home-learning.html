The Process

How It Works

Simple from start to finish — we handle the matching, you focus on your child's progress.

1
Family Registers

Parent or carer selects subject, age group, and preferred format — group class, online 1-to-1, or in-person.

2
Joybringers Matches

Your child is matched with a vetted, DBS-checked trainer suited to their needs and learning stage.

3
Sessions Delivered

Learning happens online, at our Quorum Business Park centre, or at home (for eligible families).

4
Your Payment Makes a Difference

All fees go directly to Joybringers. After trainer costs, every surplus pound funds free programmes for families who can't afford support.

Safeguarding

Your Child's Safety Comes First

Safeguarding is built into every part of this service — not added on at the end. We follow the same rigorous standards that underpin all our charitable work.

Read Our Safeguarding Policy
  • All trainers complete enhanced DBS checks and safeguarding induction
  • A parent or responsible adult must be present for all under-18 home sessions
  • Sessions take place in a communal area — never a child's bedroom
  • All visits are logged with check-in and check-out times
  • No direct private arrangements outside the Joybringers booking process
  • Trainers hold a clear code of conduct and identity verification
  • Clear reporting route to our Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL)
  • A specific Home Visit & One-to-One Tuition Policy is in place

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this the same as Joybringers' free programmes?
This is a paid service — separate from our free charitable programmes. The difference is that the income from this service helps us run and expand those free programmes. Think of it as a way to benefit your child while also helping other families who can't afford support.
How are trainers selected?
All trainers are vetted by Joybringers — this includes identity checks, references, safeguarding induction, and an enhanced DBS check where required. We only work with people whose skills and values align with ours.
Why are home visits priced higher?
A 1-hour home lesson typically uses 2 hours of a trainer's time once travel is included. To keep the service sustainable and ensure trainers are fairly paid, home visits are priced as a premium option. We recommend online or centre-based sessions as the best value choice, and 90-minute or 2-hour blocks when home visits are preferred.
Can I book a one-off session rather than a block?
We recommend 6 or 12-week blocks because they give children consistent support and make it easier to track progress. Blocks also help families plan ahead and allow us to schedule trainers effectively. Please get in touch to discuss your needs — we'll do our best to be flexible.
Who do I pay and how?
All payments go directly to Joybringers Charity — never directly to a trainer. This protects both families and trainers and ensures all income is managed transparently through our charity accounts. Payment details will be confirmed at the point of booking.
Is this service available across Newcastle and the North East?
Online sessions are available to anyone. Group and centre-based sessions run from our Quorum Business Park location (3rd Floor, The Light Box Q2, Benton Ln, NE12 8EU). Home visits are available within a 10-mile radius; please contact us for locations beyond this.
