Why Partner With Joybringers?

Partnering with us means investing in people, community and lasting positive change.

Mission-Aligned Impact

Your support directly reaches children, young people and families who need it most. Every partnership creates measurable, real-world change — and we'll report back on exactly what it achieved.

Brand Visibility & CSR

Be recognised as a champion of your community. Your organisation's logo features on our website, social media, events and printed materials — demonstrating your commitment to corporate social responsibility.

Transparent Reporting

We provide partners with regular impact reports, event invitations, photo opportunities and case studies — giving you tangible evidence of the difference your partnership makes.

Partnership Options

Choose the type of partnership that suits your organisation best — or combine more than one.

Corporate Sponsorship

  • Named sponsor recognition
  • Impact reports included
  • Gift Aid eligible options

What You Get as a Partner

Every Joybringers partnership is tailored to your organisation's goals. Here's what all partners receive:

  • Logo on Our Website & Materials

    Your logo featured prominently on our partner page, event flyers, social media posts and annual report.

  • Quarterly Impact Reports

    Receive detailed updates on what your partnership achieved — number of people reached, programmes delivered and outcomes.

  • Photo & Story Opportunities

    Attend our events, receive photography and case studies you can use in your own CSR and communications.

  • Social Media Recognition

    Regular shout-outs and acknowledgements across our Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok and Twitter platforms.

  • NCVO Network Access

    As an NCVO member charity, partnering with us connects your organisation to a wider network of charities and community organisations.

How It Works

Getting started is simple. We'll guide you every step of the way.

1
Get in Touch

Complete our partnership enquiry form below or email us at info@joybringerscharity.org. Tell us a little about your organisation and what you have in mind.

2
We Meet & Plan

We'll arrange a call or meeting to understand your goals, share our programmes and work together to design a partnership that creates genuine value for both sides.

3
We Make an Impact

Your partnership gets underway. We deliver, we report back and we celebrate the difference we've made together in the lives of young people and families.
