About Joybringers Charity

Joybringers Charity is a registered charity based in Newcastle upon Tyne, dedicated to supporting children, young people and families to build brighter futures.

We believe that every child and young person deserves the opportunity to learn, grow and reach their full potential, regardless of their background or circumstances.

Through educational programmes, digital skills training, mentoring, wellbeing activities and community events, we help people develop confidence, gain practical skills and create positive opportunities for the future.

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision

A world where every child, young person and family has the opportunity to thrive, achieve their potential and contribute positively to their community.

Our Mission

To empower children, young people and families through education, skills development, mentoring, wellbeing support and community engagement — helping everyone to Believe, Belong, and Become.

Our Values

Three words that guide everything we do.

01

Believe

We believe in the potential of every individual — no matter their background, circumstances or starting point.

02

Belong

We create welcoming, inclusive environments where everyone feels valued, respected and part of a community.

03

Become

We support people to develop their skills, achieve their goals and become the best version of themselves.

What We Do

We deliver a range of free and accessible programmes across Newcastle and the North East, supporting young people from ages 7 to 25.

Digital Skills & Coding

Coding, data analytics, R programming and digital skills training to help young people develop the tools needed for the modern world.

Employability & Career Support

CV clinics, mock interviews, career talks and confidence-building workshops to help young people and adults prepare for employment.

Mentoring & Personal Development

Guidance, positive role models and personal development support to build confidence, resilience and ambition in young people.

Community Activities

Family events, excursions, workshops, cultural activities and community projects that bring people together and strengthen local communities.

Health & Wellbeing

Promoting healthy lifestyles, positive wellbeing and opportunities for people to stay active, connected and supported.

Safeguarding

Joybringers Charity is committed to safeguarding and promoting the welfare of children and young people. We follow the statutory guidance under the Children Act 1989 and 2004.

All staff and volunteers working with children and young people undergo enhanced DBS checks. Our safeguarding policy is reviewed annually.

Concerns can be reported to: info@joybringerscharity.org

Equality & Accessibility

We are committed to equality, diversity and inclusion. We do not discriminate on the grounds of age, disability, gender, race, religion or any other protected characteristic under the Equality Act 2010.

Our programmes and communications are designed to be accessible to all. If you require information in an alternative format, please contact us.
