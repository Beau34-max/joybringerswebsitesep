Our Impact

What We Do

Joybringers is a Newcastle-based registered charity supporting children, young people and families — particularly those experiencing disadvantage or financial hardship. We combine basic-needs support with education, digital skills, employability and family engagement, so that young people are supported not only with immediate challenges but also to build confidence, independence and better long-term opportunities.

See Our Stories  

Our Programme Strands

Everything we do sits within three connected strands — Basic Needs, Education & Skills, and Family & Community.

Strand 1

Basic Needs Support

Food & Essentials Support

Every Friday · 11am–2pm Year-round All families

Food parcels, toiletries, hygiene products, household essentials and donated clothing for young people and families experiencing hardship.

Strand 2

Education & Youth Development

SATs & GCSE Preparation

Saturday mornings · Jan–May Year 6 & GCSE

Maths, English, revision support, past papers, exam techniques and confidence building.

Joybringers Future Ready

Sat afternoons · Feb–Apr Ages 18–25

Data analytics, software development, web/app development, CVs, interview preparation and mentoring.

Joybringers Discover

Sat mornings · Jun–Oct Ages 7–11

Digital creativity, simple coding, computer confidence, games, teamwork and life skills.

Joybringers Create

Sat mornings · Jun–Oct Ages 12–17

Coding, animation, games development, digital projects, creativity, teamwork and life skills.

Holiday Skills Programme

Selected school holidays Ages 7–17

Arts, crafts, crocheting, knitting, sewing, soap making, cooking, Maths and English.

Strand 3

Family & Community Engagement

ParentConnect

January, May & September Parents & carers

Online parenting sessions covering wellbeing, education, confidence, safeguarding and family issues.

Family Fun Day

Once each summer All ages

Annual community event across Tyne and Wear — games, activities, food, wellbeing, outreach and programme registration.

Annual Banquet & Awards

November Families, volunteers & partners

Certificates, awards, project showcases, recognition and celebration. The final programme of the year.

Success Stories

Real programmes. Real people. Real impact.

Digital Skills May–June 2026

6-Week R-Programming Coding Bootcamp

A beginner-friendly bootcamp introducing participants to R programming — covering data exploration, coding fundamentals, and problem-solving. Each Saturday session included hands-on challenges in a supportive environment, helping young people build confidence and practical digital skills.

The programme supports Joybringers' mission to help people Believe, Belong, and Become by equipping learners with tools for future career development.

Digital Skills February 2026

6-Week Data Analytics Bootcamp

Hands-on training for young people aged 16–25 in Excel, data cleaning, data analysis, and Power BI. Participants explored real datasets and built confidence in using data tools for decision-making and career development.

The bootcamp created a supportive space where young people were encouraged to think critically and strengthen their digital skills.

Well-being February 2026

Fruit Kebabs Workshop — Healthy Snacks Session

A fun, hands-on healthy snacks session led by Donna Robinson. Young people learnt food preparation skills, created colourful fruit kebabs, and explored healthy eating tips in a friendly, supportive environment promoting wellbeing and teamwork.

A safe space for learning, connection, and enjoyment — perfectly reflecting our mission to help young people Believe, Belong, and Become.
Digital Skills Nov–Dec 2025

6-Week Coding & Data Analysis Training

A Coding & Data Training Programme for young people aged 7–15, delivered over six weeks at our NEON Office. Participants built real projects and explored technology with growing confidence.

"My child gained creativity, self-belief, and a renewed passion for learning." — Parent
Outdoor Adventures September 2025

Youth Boat Sailing 2025

An unforgettable Youth Boat Cruise for young people aged 14–18, filled with games, new friendships, and fun memories. Spaces filled quickly — showing how eager our young people are to connect and create lasting memories together.

Milestone September 2025

Joybringers Charity New Office

Joybringers moved into our new office at Neon Quorum Business Park, NE12 8BU — strengthening our capacity to support young people across Newcastle and beyond.

Heartfelt thanks to Avire UK for making this milestone possible. Your support helps us create brighter futures.
Community August 2025

Blakelaw Family Fun Day

With support from Blakelaw Council, Joybringers hosted a vibrant Family Fun Day at Blakelaw Park. Families enjoyed dancing, barbecue, bouncy castles, and face painting in a day full of joy, connection, and community spirit.

"Please, bring this joy back to us again!" — Community Attendee
Community May 2025

Outdoor Fun Day — Carlisle Park

Over 80 people joined for sack races, football, and swimming at Carlisle Park on 31st May 2025. These community days foster confidence, friendships, and joy for families across the North East.

Multi-Skilled Workshop

"By the end of the session, I could build my own simple animation!"

Our free CV Clinic and Mock Interview Workshop featured expert speakers sharing interview and CV tips — boosting participant confidence for job readiness. Followed by an Online Career Talk on 25th May 2024.
