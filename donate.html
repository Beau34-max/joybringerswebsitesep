Other ways to support us

Can't donate online right now? There are other ways to make a difference.

Donate by Bank Transfer

Email us at info@joybringerscharity.org and we'll send you our bank details. Please include your name as the reference.

Fundraise for Us

Run, bake, or challenge yourself for Joybringers. Set up a fundraising page and share it with your friends and family. Get in touch to let us know!

Volunteer Your Skills

Your time is one of the most valuable gifts. Explore our volunteer roles and see how your skills can directly benefit our community.

Need to cancel or change your monthly gift?

Monthly donations made via PayPal can be cancelled at any time through your PayPal account: go to Settings → Payments → Manage automatic payments and find Joybringers Charity. You can cancel with no notice period and no charge. If you need help, email us and we'll assist you promptly.
